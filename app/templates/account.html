Add to your Home Screen

{{ app_name }} works like an app from your Home Screen: no browser bars, its own icon, and a page that says so when the server can't be reached.

  • iPhone and iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

The offline page needs the app on https, like your own domain. After the icon changes, remove the app from your Home Screen and add it again.

Backups

A copy of everything (cards, accounts, notes, imports, prices) is made automatically every night after the price update, and each time the app starts after an upgrade. The last 7 nightly copies, the last 5 upgrade copies and the last 10 made by hand are kept, in data/backups. Restoring makes a copy of what's there first, so it can be undone.

Your own card and chase photos aren't in these copies: Download photos saves them all in one zip.

About

Free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 · Source code ↗

Where the data comes from, each under its own terms:

  • TCGdex: sets, cards, pictures and TCGplayer prices. The set codes the scanner reads are made from TCGdex's cards database.
  • TCGCSV: TCGplayer's daily prices and product pictures.
  • CardTrader's API, when the host added a token: Near Mint prices.
  • PokéAPI: Pokémon names, the Pokédex and sprites.
  • European Central Bank: exchange rates.
  • Only while an admin has them switched on: Limitless TCG (Japanese pictures), PTCG-CHS-Datasets and PTCG-card-data (mainland Chinese cards) and Bulbapedia (descriptions, CC BY-NC-SA).
  • Picture matching: OpenAI's CLIP model, from Jina's clip-as-service.
  • The Nunito font (SIL Open Font License) and htmx (Zero-Clause BSD).
