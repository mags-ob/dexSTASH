About

Prices are kept for the cards you have, chase, follow or added a link or price to. Each printing's price is the average of every source that has one for that printing: TCGplayer's market price (else its mid) from TCGCSV, a free daily copy of TCGplayer's prices, or from TCGdex for printings TCGCSV doesn't have; CardTrader's Near Mint median with a token; and a price you typed for that printing. The range shows the lowest and highest. Cardmarket's prices aren't used; Search Cardmarket still opens its page.

CardTrader's price is the median of the Near Mint listings in the card's language (of the cheapest 25 or so its API sends); a printing with no Near Mint listing gets none, and a listing that doesn't say its language is left out. A listing for the whole card counts only for the card's one Normal or Holo printing. One request a second, through CardTrader's own API.

A downloaded price far above the others (over 20 times and over $20 more than TCGplayer's) is left out. A printing you have or chase with no price of its own uses ≈ its closest printing's, marked wherever it shows. A card you add or import is priced straight away from its set. Links you paste are saved for you to open: dexSTASH never reads TCGplayer's or PriceCharting's pages.

Keys are for sites' official APIs, so dexSTASH can read their prices without reading their pages. A key is stored encrypted with this install's secret key, in data/api_keys.json next to the database but not in it: backups, restores and exports never hold one, and it's never shown again after you save it, only its last 4 characters. About

The app only contacts a site here while it's on. Off, nothing is sent to it, not even from your phone: its pictures aren't shown. Turning a site off deletes nothing. Pictures, Chinese sets and descriptions you already have stay as they are, and are read again once you turn it back on.
